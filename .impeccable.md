## Design Context

### Users
Personal use. Individual tracking personal tasks and projects — someone who wants to stay organized without friction.

### Brand Personality
Minimal, clean, functional. Notion-inspired — stripped back, content-first, nothing decorative.
**3-word personality**: Energetic, focused, intentional.

### Emotional Goals
- **Energetic & productive**: The interface should feel fast, responsive, and get you into flow
- **Not corporate/clinical**: Avoid sterile enterprise vibes — keep it human and approachable
- **Content-first**: UI recedes, the work dominates

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light and dark modes, clean aesthetic
- **Philosophy**: Less is more. Every element must justify its existence
- **Energy level**: Minimal foundation with purposeful moments of momentum (smooth transitions, satisfying interactions)
- **Reference**: Notion — minimal, white-space driven, typography-focused
- **Anti-reference**: Corporate enterprise tools — avoid sterile, clinical vibes

### Design Principles
1. **Content over chrome** — UI recedes, content dominates
2. **Energetic minimalism** — refined interactions that feel responsive and alive
3. **Neutral palette with purpose** — tinted neutrals, color used intentionally (not decoration)
4. **Monospace for metadata** — dates, tags, labels use monospace for technical feel
5. **Flow-state friendly** — reduce friction, get out of the way, support deep work
